A completed research report will include:

1. **Metadata** - Topic, date, paper counts, data source
2. **Executive Summary** - Key findings, consensus level, evidence quality
3. **Evidence Strength Analysis** - Scored papers by quality tier
4. **Consensus vs. Dispute Mapping** - Agreement levels and intellectual fault lines
5. **Context-Dependent Effects** - Domain/method/population qualifications
6. **Heterogeneous Results** - Mixed findings and explanatory variations
7. **Methodological Tensions** - Study design debates
8. **Publication Bias Signals** - Systematic bias detection
9. **Citation Recycling Analysis** - Review overlap assessment
10. **Knowledge Topology Map** - 4D visualization of evidence landscape
11. **Recommendations** - Research gaps and improvements needed
12. **Conclusions** - Hedged claims calibrated to evidence strength
13. **Appendix** - Full paper list with scores and OpenAlex IDs

## Methodology Overview

The agent implements:

- **OpenAlex API** for academic paper retrieval (100+ papers per query)
- **Evidence Strength Scoring** (method_weight + citation_signal - uncertainty_flag)
- **Four Conflict Types** (directional, methodological, domain, uncertainty)
- **Citation Recycling Detection** to identify inflated consensus
- **Publication Bias Flagging** with epistemic humility
- **Context-Dependent Labeling** to avoid overgeneralization
- **Four-Dimensional Knowledge Topology** mapping intellectual landscape

Evidence strength formula:
```
evidence_strength = method_weight + citation_signal - uncertainty_flag

Method weights:
  Meta-analysis: +3
  RCT: +2
  Longitudinal: +1
  Observational: 0
  Theory: -1

Citation signal: min(log(citations+1) / max(1, current_year-year), 1.5)

Uncertainty penalties:
  High heterogeneity: +1.0
  Small sample: +0.5
  Replication failure: +1.0
  Conflict of interest: +0.5
```

Consensus criteria:
- ≥3 papers from ≥2 independent author groups
- Median evidence strength ≥1.0
- Directional agreement >70%

## Quality Assurance

Built-in epistemic safeguards:
1. ✓ No single-paper claims (require ≥3 papers)
2. ✓ No industry-only evidence flagging
3. ✓ No cherry-picking (report contradictions prominently)
4. ✓ No false certainty (probabilistic language by strength)
5. ✓ No citation inflation detection
6. ✓ No context collapse (qualify all claims)

Hedging language calibrated to evidence:
- **≥3.0:** "Strong evidence suggests..."
- **≥2.0:** "Moderate evidence indicates..."
- **≥1.0:** "Limited evidence supports..."
- **<1.0:** "Preliminary/weak evidence hints..."
- **<0.0:** "Evidence is insufficient/contradictory"
